Four marks for PlanPerm
The current mark is a teal rounded square with a white tick — the default SaaS checkmark. Each direction below trades on something the product actually does.
Option A · Plot boundary
planperm
Mono · reversed in a tile
An irregular plot outline with the site marked inside it. Planning is fundamentally about parcels of land, and no competitor uses this.
Tradeoff: the irregular shape needs care at 16px — the corners soften into mush in a browser tab.
Option B · Watched area
planperm
Mono · reversed in a tile
Four parcels, one filled. Reads as a map extract and as "this is the plot being watched" — which is exactly the watch agent's promise.
Tradeoff: four-square grids are common in analytics branding; it is distinctive in planning but not in software.
Option C · Received stamp
planperm
Mono · reversed in a tile
The date stamp a council applies on receipt — the moment your five-week observation window starts. It puts the deadline at the centre of the identity.
Tradeoff: a clock face reads as "scheduling" or "time tracking" to anyone who does not know the domain.
Option D · Wordmark only

No mark at all. An editorial serif wordmark with one teal full stop — the register of a land registry or a law report, which is the authority this product wants.

Recommendation — Option B. It survives 16px, works reversed in a tile, and means the one thing no competitor is selling: a specific area under watch. Option D is the strongest typographically and would pair well with B's mark as the app icon.
