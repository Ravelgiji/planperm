Design proposal

PlanPerm visual refresh

Four logo directions, a display-face swap, and a colour scale for deadlines — built on the tokens already in app.py, so nothing here needs a rebuild.

Matched to the live app: DM Sans, #0f766e green, #12383c ink, 17px metric radii, 10px buttons, the 1.15 / 3.25 / 1.35 column split. Three things change; everything else stays where it is.

01 · Identity

Four marks

The current mark is a teal rounded square with a white tick — the default SaaS checkmark. Each of these trades on something the product actually does.

Option A · Plot boundary
planperm
Mono · reversed

An irregular plot outline with the site marked inside it. Planning is fundamentally about parcels of land, and nobody in this market uses it.

Tradeoff: the irregular shape needs care at 16px — those corners soften into mush in a browser tab.

Option B · Watched area
planperm
Mono · reversed

Four parcels, one filled. Reads as a map extract and as this is the plot being watched — which is exactly what the watch agent sells.

Tradeoff: four-square grids are common in analytics branding. Distinctive in planning, not in software.

Option C · Received stamp
planperm
Mono · reversed

The date stamp a council applies on receipt — the moment your five-week observation window starts. Puts the deadline at the centre of the identity.

Tradeoff: a clock face reads as "scheduling" to anyone who does not already know the domain.

Option D · Wordmark only
planperm
planperm p Mono · favicon

No mark at all. An editorial serif wordmark with one teal full stop — the register of a land registry or a law report, which is the authority this product is claiming.

Tradeoff: nothing to use as an app icon or avatar except a single letter.

Recommendation — Option B. It survives 16px, works reversed in a tile, and means the one thing no competitor is selling: a specific area under watch. Option D is the strongest typographically, and its p tile would pair with B's mark if you want both.
02 · Typography

Replace Fraunces

DM Sans stays for the interface — it is doing its job. The display face is the problem.

05 · Effort

What each change costs

Swap the display face Two lines: the Google Fonts @import and the font-family on stMetricValue / .hero-stat strong. Minutes
New logo mark Replaces the .brand-mark CSS pseudo-element with inline SVG. Also gives you a favicon, which the app currently lacks. Minutes
Deadline urgency scale Four token triples, then keying the alert cards off days_left — which core/weekly_list.py already computes. An hour
Estimate badge One small component, used wherever a derived date appears. The clearest trust signal for the cost. An hour
Type-led hero Drops HERO_IMAGE for a gradient and a small plot diagram. Removes a remote image dependency, and the page stops depending on a stock photo for its character. An hour
